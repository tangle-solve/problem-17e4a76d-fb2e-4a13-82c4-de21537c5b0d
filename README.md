### CHEM-000108: Predictive exchange–correlation steps in charge separation
- **Subfield:** Theoretical and computational chemistry
- **Open:** Reproduce the spatial steps and derivative discontinuities that enforce correct electron allocation between weakly coupled fragments during dissociation and charge transfer. Predict their positions and heights from the system itself.
- **Status:** Recognized for decades. Analytical work in 2025 derived plateaus and investigated approximations; inexpensive general reproduction remains unresolved.
- **Type:** theory
- **Why:** Incorrect fractional charge corrupts redox energetics and charge-transfer mechanisms.
- **Key works:** N. E. Rahat and E. Kraisler (2025), *Plateaus in the Potentials of Density-Functional Theory: Analytical Derivation and Useful Approximations*, [DOI:10.1021/acs.jctc.4c01771](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jctc.4c01771); P. Mori-Sánchez and A. J. Cohen (2014), *The derivative discontinuity of the exchange-correlation functional*, [article](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24920317/).
- **Related:** Condensed-matter physics: charge localization and transport in correlated materials.
